Delete your Mentell account

Deleting removes your login, profile, money plan, daily tracker and every payment the app caught. It cannot be undone.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open the Mentell app and go to Account.
  2. Tap Delete my account, then tap again to confirm.

Without the app

Send us a WhatsApp message from the number you signed up with. We delete the account within 7 days and confirm on WhatsApp.

WhatsApp: delete my account

Kept after deletion: only the records of payments you made to Mentell (amount and date), with your name and number removed, as accounting law requires. See the privacy policy.
