Privacy policy

Mentell website (mentell.co.in) and the Mentell Android app. Last updated 7 November 2026.

Mentell is run by Noor Mohammed J, Tamil Nadu, India (contact: WhatsApp +91 96298 43122, email noormohammed.j@gmail.com). This page says, in plain words, what we keep about you, why, where, and how you remove it. We follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we keep, and why

WhatWhy
Your name and WhatsApp numberYour login, and so Noor can help you with your plan on WhatsApp.
Your passwordOnly a scrambled (hashed) form, so we can check it. Nobody at Mentell can read it.
Your money plan: EMIs, salary, house expenses, habitsTo work out your EMI-free date and saving plan, and to show it on your other phone or the website.
Daily tracker entries ("I saved", "I spent")Your streak, monthly total and the 50% launch offer.
Payments the app reads from notifications (Android app, only if you turn on Notification access): amount, who it was paid to / received from, time, which app or bank sent it, the UPI referenceTo ask you "where did this money go?" and show your month by section. We read only payment apps (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, BHIM and similar) and bank SMS notifications. We do not read chats, WhatsApp, emails or OTPs, and we never ask for your bank password, PIN or account number.
Body and mind check answersWorked out on your phone to show your result. Not sent to us unless you choose to share it.
Payments you make to Mentell (₹99 plan etc.)Through Razorpay. We receive the payment record (amount, date, your number); we never see your card or UPI PIN.
Website usage (pages visited, buttons tapped)Google Analytics, to improve the site. It does not receive your name, number or money numbers.

Where it is kept

On your phone, and in Mentell's database on Supabase (a cloud database service), protected so that each person can see only their own data. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone for advertising. Service providers who run parts of Mentell for us: Supabase (database and login), Razorpay (payments), Google (website analytics, Play Store).

How long

As long as you have an account. When you delete your account, everything about you is removed: login, profile, plan, tracker, caught payments. Records of payments you made to Mentell are kept as accounting requires, with your name and number removed.

Your choices

  • Turn off payment reading any time: Android Settings → Notifications → Device & app notifications (Notification access) → Mentell → off.
  • Delete your account: in the app, Account → Delete my account. Or see delete your account (works without the app).
  • Get a copy of your data or correct it: WhatsApp us; we reply within 7 days.

Children

Mentell is for adults (18+) managing their own money.

Health and money information

Health lines and money plans in Mentell are general information and estimates, not medical or financial advice.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date above. For big changes we tell you in the app.
